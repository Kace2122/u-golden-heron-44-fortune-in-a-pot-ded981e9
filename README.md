One kind, funny fortune for a houseplant on the windowsill, in a single line of 120 characters or fewer.
`main.py` writes the fortune to `/output/entry.txt` (deterministic).

Entry: "You will outgrow this pot, this windowsill, and every doubt your keeper ever had about their green thumb."
